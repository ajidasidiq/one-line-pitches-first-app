One sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun. It is the whole entry: no code, no explanation — just a warm, plain-spoken pitch that meets the friend where they are (sitting on an idea for months), gives them a concrete reason to say yes now (describe it in plain words and the agent builds the first version), and makes handing the idea over feel easy and low risk (no code, no cost to try). Built for the make.fun bounty **"A Day of One-Line Pitches"**.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (validated to 1–240 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace).
